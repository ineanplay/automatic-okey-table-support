# Design features and everyday use

[English](design-and-use.md) · [Türkçe](../tr/design-and-use.md) · [Deutsch](../de/design-and-use.md) · [Guide index](../README.md)

The features below explain how the described automatic OKEY mechanism supports play and maintenance. Check the selected model's configuration, controls and service instructions before purchase or use.

## From design to practical benefit

| Design feature | What it does | Practical benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Symmetrical dual-motor drive and a synchronised tile guide | Keep the shuffle tray and guide moving together | Smoother shuffling with less opposing resistance |
| Two-layer cushioning surface | Softens tile impacts into the tray | A more comfortable sound environment around the table |
| Direct magnetic drive, gear rollers and a short transport path | Move tiles through a compact pickup assembly | Smoother transport and fewer belt-related maintenance tasks in that assembly |
| Flexible two-contact pusher and spiral storage track | Distribute force along the tile row and reduce resistance | More orderly tile delivery |
| Staged lift and delivery toward the centre | Guide rows onto the playing surface | Convenient pickup and less manual pushing of delivered rows |
| Local fault diagnostics | Identify the relevant side or mechanism for inspection | Clearer information when requesting service |
| Removable dice chamber and modular tabletop | Provide access for permitted cleaning and service | Easier access when maintenance is required |
| Reinforced aluminium-composite top with four-corner positioning | Supports tabletop flatness and positioning | A stable playing surface |
| Flexible tile-lift board | Yields when it encounters an obstruction | Helps protect the lift structure from obstruction-related damage |

The flexible lift does not make it safe to place hands or objects in a moving mechanism. Follow the safety instructions and keep the movement area clear.

## Choosing features for your setting

**Homes and social rooms.** Cushioned tile handling, orderly rows and delivery toward the centre support a comfortable shared game. Check the complete model's dimensions and allow space for chairs and access.

**Cafés and OKEY clubs.** Automatic shuffling and delivery reduce repeated manual preparation between games. A simpler pickup transmission and accessible components help staff plan maintenance. Agree on cleaning responsibilities and record recurring fault codes for service.

**Shared venues.** Use one clear operating routine, the approved magnetic tile sets and the documented game mode. Keep the manual and support details available to staff. Confirm the expected usage pattern with the supplier when choosing a model.

## Maintenance access

A removable top or dice chamber makes access easier, but the user manual determines which tasks an operator may perform. Switch off and unplug before any permitted cleaning or access. Internal inspection, adjustment and repair belong to qualified service personnel.

Use the [maintenance schedule](../../../manuals/text/manual-en.md#section-10) and the [interior-maintenance instructions](../../../manuals/text/manual-en.md#section-12). Do not force a mechanism or bypass its protective features.
