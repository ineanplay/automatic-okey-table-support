# R&D measurements and what they describe

[English](rd-measurements.md) · [Türkçe](../tr/rd-measurements.md) · [Deutsch](../de/rd-measurements.md) · [Guide index](../README.md)

These selected figures summarize product R&D measurements supplied for the mechanism described in this guide. Each figure belongs to the named component or action. Comparisons refer to the conventional mechanism used in the R&D reference. Confirm the applicable model, tile configuration and measurement conditions when using a figure in a purchase specification.

## Selected technical figures

| Component or action | Reported figure | Measurement scope |
|---|---|---|
| Shuffling noise | Approximately 50 dB; conventional reference 78 dB | Noise during shuffling; preserve the measurement setup when comparing results |
| Magnetic pickup response | 0.3 seconds | Pickup response, separate from a complete shuffling and delivery cycle |
| Tile delivery time | As fast as approximately 40 seconds | Reported tile delivery time for the described mechanism; confirm the tile and program configuration |
| Pickup transport distance | Approximately one third of the conventional reference | Distance travelled within the pickup and transport assembly |
| Lift geometry | 18° + 9° | Successive lift sections, not the inclination of the whole table |
| Tile-lift action | Average 4 seconds for mainstream tile formats; conventional reference 7 seconds | The lifting action only |
| Pickup motor operating temperature | No more than 60°C during continuous operation; conventional reference 90°C | The pickup motor under the referenced operating conditions |
| Tile-lift motor power | Reduced from 20 W to 10 W | The lift motor; not total table power consumption |
| Dice-chamber removal/refitting | 1 second | The quick-release action; not a complete cleaning task |
| Tabletop removal | 3 seconds without tools | Removing the modular tabletop; not a complete service task |

## Reading the figures correctly

- Keep qualifiers such as “approximately”, “as fast as”, “average” and “no more than” with the figures.
- A component response, a lift movement and a complete delivery process measure different actions. Their timings are not interchangeable.
- Motor temperature describes a component, not a safe-to-touch surface temperature or a permitted room temperature.
- Component power does not establish the whole table's electricity consumption or running cost.
- Service-access timings do not change the required shutdown, unplugging or qualified-service procedures.

The warranty duration and service coverage remain those in the applicable sales and warranty documents.

## Comparing a specific model

For a meaningful comparison, provide the model, intended game mode and tile set. Ask for the relevant operating and measurement conditions, including the acoustic measurement setup for noise figures. This summary does not specify microphone distance or frequency weighting, so the noise values should not be treated as directly comparable with an unrelated published test.
